# Design Inspiration from Copenhagen

Traveling through Copenhagen opened my eyes to the profound impact of minimalist design on daily living. The Danish approach to interior design isn't just about aesthetics—it's a philosophy that prioritizes functionality, sustainability, and human well-being.

## The Power of Light

![Light in Copenhagen](/dehallen-01.jpg)
![Natural textures](/buiksloterham-01.jpg)

In Copenhagen, natural light is treated as a precious resource. Every window, every surface, every color choice is made with consideration for how it will interact with the limited Nordic light. This principle has fundamentally changed how I approach lighting design in my own projects.

## Key Takeaways

- **Less is always more** - Every element must earn its place
- **Natural materials** create warmth without clutter  
- **Functional beauty** - Form follows function, but both must coexist
